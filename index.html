Questions

Frequently asked

How long is each internship program?
+
Programs range from 4 to 6 weeks depending on the track. You get weekly mentorship sessions, project checkpoints, and a final review before certification.
Can complete beginners apply?
+
Absolutely. Several tracks like C, Python, and Web Development are designed for beginners. Your mentor will calibrate the difficulty to match your learning speed.
Is this program free?
+
Application is free. There may be a nominal program fee depending on the track. All details are shared after your application is reviewed.
Who are the mentors?
+
All mentors are active industry professionals - developers, data scientists, and engineers with 3–10 years of experience at product and service companies.
Will I get a real project in my portfolio?
+
Yes. Every intern ships a real project. You'll have a GitHub repo, deployment link, and documented case study to show employers at the end of the program.
How does certificate verification work?
+
Each certificate has a unique ID. Employers can visit internbite.in/verify, enter the ID, and instantly see your project outcomes and completion details.
